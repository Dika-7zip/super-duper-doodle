The game plan

How a booking becomes game day.

Three phases, start to finish. You'll talk to a real host the whole way through — not a booking form.

Pregame

Draft the format

We learn your headcount, space, and what's already failed before. You leave the call with a game, a headcount plan, and a price.

Game day

We run it, live

A Fieldhouse host arrives with the kit, the scoreboard, and the mic. You show up and play — setup and teardown are ours.

Postgame

Keep the trophy

Final standings, photos, and a highlight reel land in your inbox by morning, along with a rebooking date if you want a rematch.
